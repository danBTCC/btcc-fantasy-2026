# 2027 Idea — Fantasy Teams Refresh

## Origin

This idea came from Jake’s 2026 end-of-season questionnaire response.

When asked about Fantasy Teams, Jake said:

> Add more reason to keep track of fantasy teams and care about who you're in a team with.

The current Fantasy Teams championship works, but players may not have much reason to actively follow their team, know how their teammates are performing, or feel attached to the team they have been placed in.

The aim for 2027 would be to create more team identity and give players reasons to care about both individual event results and the season-long Fantasy Teams championship.

---

## 1. Teams Choose Their Own Names

For 2026 the team names were pre-selected:

- Track Limits, Optional
- Reverse Drawn
- Yellow Flagged It
- The Tyre Stackers
- The Kerb Crawlers
- The Gravel Traps

For 2027, each team could choose its own name before the season begins.

Existing names could still be kept if the members like them.

### Purpose

This gives players more ownership of their team and helps make the Fantasy Teams championship feel like something they are actually part of rather than simply another standings table.

---

## 2. Team Captain

Each Fantasy Team would have a captain.

The captain could either:

- be chosen by the three team members;
- be randomly selected;
- or rotate during the season.

The captain should not control anybody else's driver selections.

Their role would instead be connected specifically to the Fantasy Teams competition.

---

## 3. Event-by-Event Team Winners

As well as the season-long Fantasy Teams standings, every event would have a winning Fantasy Team.

This means teams further down the overall standings would still have something to compete for at every event.

### Proposed Reward

The three members of the highest-scoring Fantasy Team at an event each receive:

**+£0.05 temporary budget for the following event.**

Example:

> Team A wins Event 2.  
> All three Team A members receive +£0.05 effective budget for Event 3.

The bonus would apply only to the following event and would then disappear.

If the team wins again, they earn another +£0.05 bonus for the next event.

### Why Temporary?

Making the bonus temporary avoids successful teams building an increasingly large permanent financial advantage.

£0.05 should be enough to make an event win worth chasing without significantly distorting the main Individual Championship.

It would also create a visible reminder on the submission page:

> **Fantasy Team Bonus: +£0.05 — Your team won the previous event 🏆**

---

## 4. Captain's Player Nomination

Before each event, the team captain could nominate one member of their team.

If that player performs particularly well during the fantasy weekend, the team earns bonus points towards the Fantasy Teams Championship.

Possible structure:

- Nominated player wins a Fantasy Race: **+5 team points**
- Nominated player wins the overall Fantasy Event: **+10 team points**
- Maximum nomination bonus per event: **10 points**

These points would apply only to the Fantasy Teams standings and would not alter the nominated player's Individual Championship score.

### Possible Restriction

To create a genuine strategic decision, the same player could not be nominated at consecutive events.

This prevents a captain simply selecting the team's strongest player every weekend.

Another option would be requiring all three team members to be nominated before somebody can be selected again.

This would need further discussion as the rule should remain simple.

---

## 5. Team of the Weekend Recognition

The winning team could receive more visible recognition around the site.

For example:

> **TEAM OF THE WEEKEND**  
> The Gravel Traps — 684 pts

This could appear on:

- Home;
- Results;
- News/weekend review;
- Fantasy Teams standings.

Recognition may be just as important as the financial reward in encouraging players to follow the competition.

---

## Strategic Effect

This could create an interesting situation where players are simultaneously teammates and rivals.

For example, two players could be fighting each other for position in the Individual Championship while also needing each other to score strongly so their Fantasy Team can win the event.

This creates another layer of strategy without interfering with the main Individual Championship.

---

## Proposed Structure

A possible 2027 Fantasy Teams system could therefore be:

1. Three players per Fantasy Team.
2. Teams choose their own names.
3. Each team has a captain.
4. The season-long Fantasy Teams Championship continues.
5. Each event also has a winning Fantasy Team.
6. Members of the event-winning team receive +£0.05 temporary budget for the next event.
7. The captain nominates one teammate before each event.
8. Strong performance from the nominated player earns bonus Fantasy Team points.
9. Team event winners receive visible recognition around the website.

---

## Things To Decide

Before introducing this for 2027, decide:

- How captains are selected.
- Whether captains remain for the whole season or rotate.
- Exact nomination bonus points.
- Whether a nominated player can be selected at consecutive events.
- Whether £0.05 is the correct event-winning reward.
- How team names are submitted and approved.
- Whether players should remain in the same Fantasy Team all season.
- Whether any of these elements should be put to a player poll.
