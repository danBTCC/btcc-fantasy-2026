# BTCC Fantasy League 2026 End of Season Questionnaire

## Purpose

- Keep the questionnaire to a maximum of 12 numbered questions.
- Some questions may contain several closely related parts, but they should still feel like one question or page to the player.
- Gather useful information for planning the 2027 Fantasy League without turning this into a long product survey.
- Where possible, only show written follow-up boxes when relevant, such as when a player selects **Change**, **Other**, or indicates that something did not work well.
- Use questionnaire responses to inform later 2027 proposal polls rather than trying to settle every rule directly.

## Notes for the Local Visual Prototype

- Build the first version as a local visual prototype only.
- Do not add Firestore questionnaire writes, response persistence, admin tracking, or production data integration at this stage.
- Do not commit, push, or deploy the questionnaire until the local design has been reviewed and explicitly approved.
- Keep the player-facing questionnaire to 12 numbered screens or cards.
- Keep multiple parts visually grouped under the same question number.
- Use temporary local prototype data rather than the player's real league identity or UID.
- Demonstrate the intended save-and-resume experience visually without implementing persistence.
- Only show conditional questions and comment boxes when relevant.
- Keep the questionnaire mobile-first and quick to complete.
- Target a completion time of around 10 minutes.

## Notes for a Later Approved Build

- Use the player's normal league identity or UID when saving responses.
- Allow progress to be saved before final submission.
- Feed questionnaire responses into later 2027 proposal polls rather than treating this questionnaire as the final decision on every rule.
